Apurímac River Expedition

The Apurímac River is one of the most famous rafting destinations near Cusco. Navigate narrow canyons barely wider than a toothpick and admire breathtaking views of the Andean landscape along the way.

This four-day expedition begins in a narrow canyon surrounded by towering granite walls. Tackle challenging Class III and IV rapids before facing the thrilling Class V rapids toward the end of the trip.

Our private cooks will prepare nourishing meals throughout the journey. At night, camp along the riverbanks, gaze at the stars and the Milky Way, and fall asleep to the soothing sound of the river.

  • Duration: 4 days
  • Difficulty: Advanced
  • Rapids: Class III, IV, and V
  • Best for: Experienced rafters

Willcamayu / Urubamba River Adventure

The Willcamayu is an excellent choice for travelers looking to experience white water rafting in Peru without extensive experience. This route offers a balance of peaceful stretches where you can paddle at a relaxed pace and more exciting sections that add a little extra challenge.

Originating high in the Vilcanota Valley, the Willcamayu winds through the Andes and around the magnificent region of Machu Picchu. Along the journey, you'll enjoy breathtaking views of towering mountain peaks covered in lush vegetation.

Also known as the Sacred River, the Willcamayu carries a special sense of magic and history that makes this rafting experience truly unforgettable.

  • Duration: Half-day or full-day options
  • Difficulty: Beginner-friendly to intermediate
  • Scenery: Andean mountains and lush vegetation
  • Best for: First-time rafters and nature lovers

Chili River Adventure

Located just minutes from the city of Arequipa, the Chili River is a fantastic destination for travelers looking for an exciting half-day white water rafting experience. With Class II and III rapids, this adventure combines thrilling sections of white water with beautiful views of the surrounding Andean landscape.

As you navigate the river, you'll encounter exciting rapids such as "La Recta del Diablo" and "El Fun Run," where teamwork and quick reactions make every moment memorable. The Chili River offers an exciting way to explore Peru beyond the city.

  • Duration: Half day
  • Difficulty: Beginner-friendly to intermediate
  • Rapids: Class II and III
  • Location: Arequipa, Peru
  • Best for: Adventure seekers and first-time rafters
